The Menu

What's always here, and what's cooking today.

Our daal and sabji are 100% gluten-free by recipe, and we make our roti, paratha, and other wheat-flour dishes in a separate dedicated space to help avoid cross-contact. We're not a certified gluten-free facility, so if you have celiac disease or a severe gluten allergy, let our staff know and we'll help you choose safely.

About Us

Cooked by two people who know what a long day needs.

Before Punjabi Tadka, our parents spent years as long-haul truck drivers, the kind of job that teaches you fast what a meal actually has to do. It has to be fresh. It has to fill you up. It has to be good for you, because you're the one sitting for hours after you eat it. And it has to taste like something worth pulling over for.

So when they opened the kitchen in 2017, they cooked the same way for everyone else: real portions, real ingredients, nothing sitting around. It's why our prices are built the way they are, too. We'd rather feed the community well than squeeze the last dollar out of a plate.
